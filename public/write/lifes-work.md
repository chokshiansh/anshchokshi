# Life's Work

*By [Ansh Chokshi](https://anshchokshi.com) · Aug 2, 2026 · 2 min read*

Canonical: https://anshchokshi.com/write/lifes-work

---

3 am thoughts

I once watched a podcast where Jensen Huang said, "When I'm not working, I'm thinking about working. And when I'm working, I'm working. I sit through movies, but I don't remember them."

At the time, I took it lightly.

Two months into building Mireye, I am living it. Even in my sleep, I am dreaming about work.

It is not healthy. I would not say I am happy either. But it is the most meaningful thing I have ever done. Some days I am suffering. Some days I am enduring. Some days I feel like I will conquer the biggest mountain ever.

The hardest part is the people.

July was the month of birthdays. Almost every day, someone I care about turned a year older. I did not remember to wish a single one.

If your birthday passed without a call from me, it was not you. I am sorry. I am still learning how to carry both.

I have stopped living the way I used to. I wear the same three outfits. I eat the same meals. I have not opened Robinhood in months. I don't remember the last time I tried something new, but I remember my customers' phone numbers and their kids' names.

The cravings I once had are gone. Not suppressed. Gone.

You cannot live like this without a reason.

Last week in group office hours, we talked about exactly that. The intrinsic reason behind why each of us wants to build a company. Most answers were similar. A chip on the shoulder. Someone to prove wrong.

I sat there and realized I have no one.

No one has ever told me I couldn't do it. Everyone I have crossed paths with has believed in me, even when it sounded delusional. I am grateful for that.

My reason was never to prove anything.

I am in a phase of life where I am proud of how far I have come, but at the same time I feel there is a long way to go. I have felt that way since I was a kid.

I think differently now about what Jensen said. He was not describing a work ethic. ***He was describing what happens when you find the thing that gets all of you.***

***I found mine.***
